Biomedical Engineering, Focused on Human Movement

Irymia is a biomedical engineering initiative dedicated to biomechanics, orthopaedics, and medical rehabilitation. We develop engineering concepts, analytical models, and prototype solutions that bridge clinical challenges and technical reasoning.

Focus Areas

Biomechanics

Human motion analysis, force mechanics, musculoskeletal modeling.

Orthopaedics

Joint mechanics, assistive devices, structural analysis.

Rehabilitation

Functional recovery, movement optimization, engineering support tools.

About Irymia

Irymia is not a commercial company. It is a collaborative platform for biomedical engineers to explore ideas, document projects, and present engineering-driven solutions in the medical domain.
